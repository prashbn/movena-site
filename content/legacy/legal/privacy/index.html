Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 September 2026

This policy explains what personal information Movena collects, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. It covers the Movena iOS and Android member apps, the member and gym web surfaces, and the movena.com.au website.

1. Who we are

The Movena platform and the Movena member apps are operated by Movena Pty Ltd (ACN 700 863 618) ("Movena", "we", "us"), in Australia. Movena provides the platform to gyms and fitness businesses ("your gym") and to their members.

  • Privacy and legal enquiries: info@movena.com.au
  • Product support: support@movena.com.au

2. How your account is created

The Movena member apps do not offer account creation or sign-up. You cannot register for Movena inside the iOS or Android app — the apps let you sign in to an account that already exists.

  • Accounts and gym access are provisioned through an authorised gym or operator workflow. Your gym sets up or links your access.
  • Because of this, we may receive your account and membership information from your gym rather than directly from you.
  • Authorised gym staff may provision a new member record, or link an existing Movena account to their gym, so your access works when you arrive. Linking is recorded in our audit logs, and we may notify you that your account has been linked.
  • Your global Movena identity details (such as your name, email, phone, date of birth and gender) are not silently overwritten by the gym-linking process.
  • We do not tell a gym that your email address already exists in another gym's Movena context.
  • Setting or resetting your password happens through a secure link we email you, which opens in your browser.

We give you notice and privacy choices through the apps, this policy, and the collection notices and consent screens shown at the relevant time.

3. Movena and your gym — who does what

Two organisations are usually involved in your information, and it helps to know which does what.

  • Your gym determines and manages your gym membership, classes, coaching, billing arrangements and gym-specific records, and decides which of its staff need access.
  • Movena provides the platform and independently manages platform identity, authentication, security, support and the operation of the service.
  • Your information falls into two broad groups: your global Movena identity and account, and your gym-specific memberships, bookings, messages, waivers, programs and records.
  • Some requests about information your gym manages may need to be handled together with your gym. Movena remains responsible for requests about information we control.

4. The information we collect

We collect what the platform needs to work. We do not use advertising trackers, advertising identifiers, or third-party behavioural analytics in our apps, and we do not track or profile you across other apps or websites.

Identity and contact details

  • Your name and email address (your sign-in identity), and whether your email has been verified.
  • Your phone number, date of birth and gender, where held.
  • An authentication token, kept securely on your device to keep you signed in.

Gym links, roles and membership

  • The gym or gyms you belong to, your member record and status, your roles or access at that gym, and your membership plan or pack (name, price, billing interval, period progress).
  • Contact details your gym holds for your membership, which may include your postal address.

Emergency contact

  • The name, relationship and phone number of an emergency contact, where provided. This is personal information about both you and the person you nominate. It may be provided by you, your parent or guardian, or your gym, and we use it for safety and emergency purposes. Please only nominate someone who is willing to be contacted.

Bookings, attendance and activity

  • Classes and sessions you book, booking and waitlist status, attendance records, cancellations, and any session feedback you give.

Payments and billing

  • Purchase and payment records — what you bought, amount, currency, date, status, and refunds.
  • Payment-card information is entered directly into Stripe, our payment processor. Movena does not receive or store your complete card number or card security code. Movena may retain limited payment and billing information, such as transaction details, card brand, last four digits, expiry date and billing contact information.

Messages

  • Messages between you and your gym's staff, including message content, read state, and any mute or block settings. Movena is a gym-to-member channel — there is no member-to-member messaging, and messages do not carry attachments.
  • If you report a message, we keep the report and a snapshot of the reported content as a safety and moderation record.
  • Messages are stored by Movena in order to provide the messaging service. The mobile apps do not keep your message history stored on your device. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted. Authorised gym staff, and Movena personnel where required to provide, support, secure or moderate the service, may access messages.

Training, performance and body measurements

  • Workouts and programs assigned to you, the workouts and sets you log, your personal records and progress history, and goals or achievements.
  • Body measurements where you or your coach record them (for example weight, body-fat percentage and body circumference measurements such as chest, waist, hips, arm or thigh).

Waivers and health intake

  • Waivers you sign, your signature image and the generated waiver document, and your answers to health-screening questions (for example pre-exercise readiness questions). These answers are health information.
  • Records that establish the signing — the date and time, the wording you agreed to, and the IP address and device information used to sign.
  • Notes your gym's staff record about your training, injuries or health considerations, where relevant to delivering your training safely.

Profile photo

  • If you set a profile photo, we store the image you choose. On both apps the photo is selected through the operating system's photo picker, resized on your device, and location data embedded in the original image is not retained.

Health and fitness data from Apple Health and Health Connect

  • Optional, and only if you turn it on — see section 6.

Device, notification and technical information

  • App version, platform and a per-request reference used for support and debugging.
  • If you allow notifications, a notification token together with your device model, language and time zone, so notifications reach the right device.
  • Crash and diagnostic information may be handled by your device platform (Apple or Google).

Authentication, security and audit records

  • Sign-in and sign-in-attempt records, including IP address, approximate country, device and app version, used to keep accounts secure, detect suspicious activity and prevent abuse. We may derive an approximate country from an IP address to help detect suspicious sign-ins and protect accounts.
  • Audit records of significant actions in the platform (for example when a member record is created or linked, or a role is granted), used for accountability and security.
  • Records of consents and agreements you give, including the wording you were shown.

Support, enquiries and website information

  • If you email us for support or a privacy request, we hold that correspondence and your contact details.
  • If you submit an enquiry form on a gym's Movena enquiry page or on movena.com.au, we collect the details you provide (such as name, email and phone) so the gym can respond.
  • Our web pages are delivered through a network provider, which processes technical request information (such as IP address) to deliver the page and protect against attacks. Our website pages load a web font from Google Fonts, which involves a request from your browser to Google.
  • Optional website analytics: if you choose "Allow analytics" on movena.com.au, the website loads Google Analytics to help us understand which pages are useful and whether the website is working as intended. Google Analytics may process information such as pages viewed, referring page, approximate location, device and browser details, and consented website interactions. We do not send the contents of your enquiry form, your name, email address, phone number or business name to Google Analytics.
  • Google Analytics remains off until you allow it. If you allow analytics, Google Analytics may use first-party cookies such as `_ga`. We store your analytics preference in your browser so the website can remember it. You can change your choice at any time through "Analytics settings" in the website footer.

We use your information to:

  • Administer gym access and services on behalf of, and together with, your gym.
  • Authenticate you and keep the service secure — including detecting suspicious sign-ins, preventing fraud and abuse, and maintaining audit records.
  • Fulfil transactions — process purchases and payments, keep a renewal card on file where you choose to, and issue receipts.

  • Provide support when you contact us.
  • Comply with legal obligations and to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.
  • Obtain your consent where collecting sensitive information, or using a device permission, requires it — for example health information and Apple Health or Health Connect access.
  • Understand and improve our public website, where you have chosen to allow website analytics.

We do not rely on consent for everything we do. Some processing is necessary to provide the service you or your gym have arranged, or to meet a legal obligation. Where we do rely on consent — such as for health information, optional health-app access or optional sharing with your gym — you can withdraw it as described in this policy.

6. Apple Health (HealthKit) and Google Health Connect

Movena includes an optional fitness feature that can read a small, specific set of health measurements from Apple Health (HealthKit) on iOS or Google Health Connect on Android, to show you how hard a completed session was.

What we request

If you turn this on, Movena requests read-only access to exactly three types of information, for the period of a session you booked and attended:

  • Heart rate
  • Active energy burned (active calories)
  • Resting heart rate

Movena does not write any data to Apple Health or Health Connect, and does not request any other health category.

What stays on your device, and what is uploaded

  • Stays on your device as part of this health integration: the raw heart-rate readings, your resting heart rate, and the age value used in the session calculation. Movena does not upload these items through Apple Health or Health Connect. Movena may separately hold your date of birth as account information, as described in section 4.
  • Uploaded to Movena, only if you also turn on sharing with your gym: a calculated summary of that session — session duration, average and peak heart rate, active energy burned, minutes in each heart-rate zone, an intensity percentage and band, intensity and training-load scores, and a data-quality indicator.
  • Visible to your gym: that calculated session summary, so the coach who trains you can see how the session went. Your raw health readings are not shared with your gym.

Your control

  • Access is optional. The apps work fully without it, and you are shown an explanation before any permission is requested.
  • Permission is requested through the operating system's own permission flow (Apple Health or Health Connect), only when you choose to connect — never at sign-in.
  • Uploading the session summary requires a separate, optional consent to share aggregated health insights with that gym, which you can turn off in the app at any time.
  • You can revoke Apple Health or Health Connect access at any time in your device settings (or by turning the connection off in Movena).
  • Revoking access stops future reads. It does not automatically delete information that was previously imported or uploaded.
  • You can request deletion of previously uploaded health summaries by emailing info@movena.com.au, subject to any information we must lawfully retain.

We treat health and fitness information as sensitive. What we do not do with health and fitness information:

  • We do not sell it.
  • We do not use it for advertising.
  • We do not use it for insurance eligibility.
  • We do not use it for employment eligibility.
  • We do not use it for unrelated marketing.
  • We do not provide medical advice or diagnosis.

7A. Connecting an AI assistant to your Movena data

You can choose to connect your Movena account to an AI assistant — today, that is ChatGPT, operated by OpenAI. This is off unless you turn it on, it is your choice rather than your gym's, and you can turn it off at any time.

You must be 18 or older to connect an assistant. Movena accounts are open to members aged 16 and over (section 11), but this particular choice is not. We work that out from the date of birth held on your Movena account, and if we do not hold one, or it is not usable, you cannot connect — we do not guess. If you are 18 or older and the option is unavailable, you can update your date of birth in your Movena profile. Your date of birth and your age are used only to answer this yes-or-no question: they are never sent to the assistant, and they do not appear in the record we keep of your consent.

Nothing is shared until you approve it. When you connect, Movena shows you an approval screen naming exactly what would be shared and who receives it. You tick the categories you want. Ticking nothing shares nothing.

What can be shared, and for how far back

My training. Your personal records other than heart rate, and your workout log — sets, reps, load, duration and distance. Where a result is linked to an exercise in Movena's own exercise catalogue, we include its catalogue reference and display name. We also include the available values, if any, for movement modality, primary movement pattern, laterality and default tracking method. A result for an exercise created by your gym is still included. If it has no Movena catalogue lineage, it is sent without an exercise name; Movena never sends the gym-authored name. How far back: personal records, no date cutoff; workout log, the last 30 days.

My session health. For sessions your gym recorded with a wearable: the date, how long you trained, how many minutes you spent in each heart-rate zone, how hard the session was overall, a training-load figure, and how complete the wearable's readings were. How far back: the last 30 days.

Why: so the assistant can answer questions about your own training when you ask it to. Movena sends this data in response to your question, not on a schedule and not in the background.

What is never shared through this connection

  • Your actual heart rate — not your average, peak or resting heart rate, and never the raw readings from your watch. Session health describes the shape of a session, not your physiology.
  • Your heart-rate personal records.
  • Calories burned, the intensity percentage, the intensity score and the number of readings the calculation used. The training-load score for a session IS shared, as described above.
  • Notes your coach wrote about you.
  • Your name, email address, phone number or address.
  • Your gym's own names for exercises, workouts, classes and sessions. If a gym-created exercise has Movena catalogue lineage, Movena sends the corresponding Movena catalogue reference and display name; otherwise the result travels unnamed.
  • Anything about other members.
  • Your daily health information — steps, sleep and resting heart rate — which is a separate choice that does not exist yet and would require your fresh consent if it ever does.

What your gym can and cannot see

Your gym's own connection to an assistant is separate and cannot reach any member's personal information. It answers questions about the gym's own booking activity — counts and totals for locations the gym approved. A gym being connected does not give it access to your data, and your connection does not give it anything either.

What happens to your data once it reaches the assistant

This is the part worth reading twice.

  • The assistant is operated by another company, not by Movena.
  • Once your data reaches your account with that company, they hold it, under your plan with them, your settings there, and their policies. Movena cannot see, change or delete it there.
  • Turning a category off, or disconnecting, stops Movena sending any more. It does not remove what has already been sent to a conversation. Those messages stay in your account with that company until you remove them there.

Disclosure outside New South Wales and overseas

The information you approve is disclosed to a company you chose, and that disclosure leaves New South Wales. Member-approved information may be transferred outside New South Wales and processed outside Australia — for ChatGPT, OpenAI's published privacy policy states that it processes and stores personal data in its facilities and servers in the United States, and in countries where its affiliates, partners or service providers are located.

This is what you are consenting to when you turn a category on. For the current ChatGPT connection, the approval screen says:

By turning on a category, you consent to Movena disclosing the selected information to ChatGPT (OpenAI). The information may be transferred outside New South Wales and processed outside Australia. Where it is disclosed overseas, you are expressly informed that Australian Privacy Principle 8.1 will not apply to that disclosure. Movena will not be accountable under the Australian Privacy Act for the recipient's subsequent handling, and you will not be able to seek redress from Movena under that Act for the recipient's conduct. The recipient handles the information under your account terms, settings and its privacy policy.

Movena remains responsible for obtaining your consent, authenticating the request, keeping the information secure while it is with Movena, and sending only the categories you approved. Turning a category off or disconnecting stops future sharing but cannot remove information already sent to the recipient.

Why we can rely on your consent for this. Because you direct the disclosure to a recipient you chose, Movena relies on your consent under the NSW Health Records and Information Privacy Act (HPP 14) for the transfer outside New South Wales, and on the informed-consent exception in Australian Privacy Principle 8.2(b) where the recipient receives or processes the information outside Australia. That exception applies only because you are expressly informed, before you consent, that APP 8.1 will not apply — which is why the sentence above is shown to you, in those words, every time a category is offered, and recorded with your consent exactly as you read it.

What this does and does not change. It concerns the recipient's *subsequent* handling of the information after you have directed the disclosure. It does not reduce Movena's own obligations to you — under the Privacy Act, under the Australian Consumer Law, for the security of information while it is with us, for notifying you of a data breach, or for any claim arising from Movena sending information you did not approve. Nothing here asks you to give up those rights.

Who the assistant's operator is, in privacy terms

When you connect an assistant, you choose the recipient and you direct the disclosure. You hold the account with that company, you decide whether to connect, and Movena sends your data only because you asked us to. In privacy terms the operator is a user-directed recipient — a party you directed us to disclose to — not a supplier Movena has engaged to process your information on our behalf, and not someone we have appointed to handle your data for our own purposes.

That is a statement about *their* side of the line, not a reduction of ours. Movena remains responsible for obtaining your authorisation properly, for sending only the categories you approved, for checking that a request really comes from you, for keeping the data secure while it is with us, and for transmitting it accurately.

What Movena remains responsible for

Naming the limits of our control is not a way of disowning our own duties. We remain responsible for collecting this information, keeping it secure, checking that a request really comes from you, and sending only what you approved.

The record we keep

When your data is disclosed to an assistant we record the fact of it — which categories, to whom, and when. We do not record the contents. A log detailed enough to reconstruct your training or health history would be a second, quieter copy of the information this feature is careful about.

These records let Movena demonstrate what was approved and when disclosures occurred. Your connections page shows what you are sharing now and lets you change it; it is not a history of individual disclosures.

We also keep a record of what you approved: the categories you chose, the exact text you were shown, the version of that text, and the versions of this Privacy Policy and of our Terms of Service that were in force when you approved.

How long we keep it: seven years. Consent and disclosure records are kept for seven years after the connection ends or your account is closed, whichever is later. These records exist to answer a question that tends to be asked long after the fact — *did this person agree, to what, and when* — and a record that has already been deleted cannot answer it for either of us.

If you ask us to delete your personal information, we still remove your identifying details from these records: your identity is scrubbed and replaced with a key that keeps the history correlatable without naming you, and what remains is the minimum needed as legal evidence — which category, to whom, when, and under which policy and terms versions. Nothing in these records contains what was actually sent.

Turning it off

Manage or remove the connection at any time from your Movena connections page. You can turn off a single category and keep the other, or disconnect entirely, which also cancels the assistant's access credential immediately.

Not advice

Anything the assistant tells you about your training is its answer, not Movena's. It is not medical advice.

8. Access, correction and deletion

Contact info@movena.com.au to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. We may need to verify your identity and may coordinate with your gym where the request concerns information managed by that gym. We will respond within a reasonable period.

You can also ask us to restrict or object to certain processing, or withdraw an optional consent, where that right applies to you.

Some things to know about deletion:

  • Deleting information Movena holds does not by itself cancel your gym membership or your payment obligations. Those arrangements are with your gym — please speak to them about ending a membership.
  • Some records may need to be retained — for example financial and payment records, waivers, and safety, fraud, audit, security or dispute records.
  • We will tell you where information cannot be deleted, and why.

You can also manage some things yourself: change or remove your profile photo in the app, turn notifications off in your device settings, turn optional health sharing off in the app, and revoke Apple Health or Health Connect access in your device settings.

9. How long we keep information

How long we keep information depends on what it is and why we hold it.

  • Sign-in and sign-in-attempt records: kept for up to 365 days.
  • Backups: information may remain in encrypted or protected backups for a limited period after it is deleted from active systems, before those backups are overwritten or expire under our normal backup practices.
  • Financial, payment and company records: retained for the period required by law (for example tax and accounting obligations).
  • Waivers, consents and safety or moderation records: retained as records of what was agreed or reported.
  • AI assistant consent and disclosure records: kept for seven years after the connection ends or the account is closed, whichever is later, as described in section 7A.
  • Other personal information: retained while reasonably required to operate the service, preserve your training or transaction history, support your gym relationship, resolve disputes, investigate fraud or safety issues, or meet our legal obligations.

When information is no longer reasonably required, we delete or de-identify it. Not every category is covered by an automated deletion schedule — where you want information removed sooner, contact info@movena.com.au and we will assess the request as described in section 8.

10. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information, including encryption in transit and, where applicable, at rest; access controls and gym scoping, so staff access is limited to their own gym and to the members and information their role requires; secure authentication and session handling; audit and security logging; protected storage; and backup and recovery measures.

Your payment-card information is collected directly by our payment processor, so your complete card number and security code are not received or stored by Movena.

No online service can be completely secure. If we become aware of a data breach that is likely to cause serious harm, we will respond in line with our obligations under Australian privacy law.

11. Minimum age, and members aged 16 or 17

You must be at least 16 years old to hold a Movena member account. Members aged 16 or 17 may use Movena where the account has been arranged through their gym and a parent or legal guardian has provided the required consent. The member uses their own gym-provisioned Movena account. A parent or guardian may contact Movena about the member's personal information, and we may verify their authority before responding.

As with all members, a member aged 16 or 17 cannot create a Movena account through the app. The gym is responsible for obtaining and retaining evidence of guardian consent through its onboarding process.

Connecting an AI assistant is 18+. A member aged 16 or 17 holds a full Movena account and uses the app normally; they cannot connect their Movena data to an AI assistant (section 7A). Guardian consent does not unlock it — the decision to send your own training and health data to another company is not one we ask a minor, or a guardian on their behalf, to make on a consent screen. Where we do not hold a usable date of birth, the connection is refused rather than allowed.

If your complaint relates to Australian privacy law and you are not satisfied with our response, you may raise it with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au, where applicable.
