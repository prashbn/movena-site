Help & Support

Last updated: 2 August 2026

Help with the Movena iOS and Android member apps — who to contact, and what your gym handles rather than us.

Contact
  • Product support: support@movena.com.au — the app, sign-in, notifications, anything not working.
  • Privacy and legal: info@movena.com.au — your personal information, access, correction or deletion requests.

Include the email address you sign in with and, if you can, your gym's name — it lets us find your account without a round trip.

Your gym handles some things, we handle others

Movena provides the platform; your gym runs your membership. Knowing which is which saves time.

  • Your gym: membership plans and pricing, class schedules, cancellation rules, attendance, coaching, pausing or ending a membership, and refunds.
  • Movena: the apps themselves, signing in, password resets, notifications, and how your personal information is handled.

If you are not sure, write to support@movena.com.au and we will point you the right way.

Signing in

You cannot create a Movena account in the app. Accounts are set up by your gym, and the apps let you sign in to an account that already exists. If you do not have access yet, your gym arranges it.

  • Forgotten password: choose Forgot password on the sign-in screen. We email you a secure link that opens in your browser.
  • No email arriving: check your spam folder first, then confirm with your gym which address your account uses.
  • Wrong gym, or no gym showing: your gym needs to link your access. Ask them, or contact us and we will check.

Bookings and classes

Book and cancel from the Book tab. Cancellation windows, waitlists and late-cancellation rules are set by your gym, not by Movena, so your gym is the right place to ask about a specific booking.

Payments, invoices and receipts

Membership and class-pack payments are processed by Stripe. Movena never sees or stores your full card details. Your invoices and receipts are in the app under Membership and Payments. For a question about an amount charged, a refund, or a plan change, contact your gym.

Messages, reporting and blocking

Messaging in Movena is a private channel between you and your gym's staff. There is no member-to-member messaging and nothing you write is public.

  • Report a message: open the conversation and press and hold the message you want to report. Your gym reviews reports and can hide the message or stop the sender messaging you.
  • Block someone: open the conversation, tap the actions button in the top bar, then Block.
  • Mute: same menu — stops notifications without blocking.

If something needs urgent attention, or there is no one at your gym who can act, email support@movena.com.au.

Health data

Connecting Apple Health or Health Connect is entirely optional, and the rest of Movena works whether or not you do. Movena never asks for health access when you sign in — only when you choose to connect.

  • To connect: open a completed session, or go to Settings → Connected health.
  • To change or turn it off (iPhone): Health app → Sharing → Apps → Movena.
  • To change or turn it off (Android): Health Connect → App permissions → Movena.

Your raw heart-rate readings stay on your device. Only a small derived summary of a session is shared with your coach, and only while you keep health sharing switched on.

Notifications

Movena asks permission before sending notifications, and you can change your mind at any time in your device settings. Turning notifications off does not affect anything else in the app.

Your personal information

To access, correct or delete your personal information, email info@movena.com.au. Our Privacy Policy explains what we collect, why, and who it is shared with.
