Connect Movena to an AI assistant

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Movena works with Claude, Anthropic's AI assistant, and ChatGPT, OpenAI's. Once connected, the assistant can report on your gym's bookings, explain how to do things in Movena and, only if you choose, summarise your own training.

What you can ask

  • Owners and Managers: weekly booking health across the locations you approve (bookings, occupancy, waitlists, capacity and fill for classes, personal training and open gym), and a daily trend from two weeks back to two weeks ahead, with upcoming sessions that still have places and links back to Movena.
  • Coaches: bookings for the sessions assigned to you.
  • Anyone with a staff role: step-by-step answers from Movena's reviewed help articles.
  • Members aged 18 or over: a summary of your own training (personal records and the last 30 days of workout results) and, separately, your session health (duration, time in heart-rate zones, intensity, training load and data quality for the last 30 days).

Before you connect

  • For booking reports and help: an Owner, Manager, Coach or Staff role at a gym that uses Movena.
  • For your own training: a Movena member account, aged 18 or over, with a date of birth on your profile and the current Movena Terms accepted. Your date of birth is used only to check your age and is never sent to the assistant.

Connect Claude

  1. In Claude, open Settings → Connectors, find Movena and choose Connect. If Movena isn't listed yet, choose Add custom connector and enter https://app.movena.com.au/api/mcp.
  2. Sign in to Movena.
  3. Movena shows an approval screen naming Claude and the address it returns to (claude.ai). Gym team members choose the locations to share; members choose My training and/or My session health. Every choice starts unticked. Choose Approve.
  4. You're back in Claude. Try “How is this week booking?” or “How do I set up a class in Movena?”

Connect ChatGPT

  1. In ChatGPT, find the Movena app and choose Connect.
  2. Sign in to Movena.
  3. Movena shows an approval screen naming ChatGPT and the address it returns to. Make the same choices as above (each starts unticked) and choose Approve.
  4. You're back in ChatGPT. Ask a question about your gym or about Movena.

What is shared, and what isn't

  • Booking reports contain counts and totals only: no member or coach names, contact details, attendance lists, session titles, descriptions or notes.
  • A member summary contains only the categories you ticked. It never includes your name, contact details, date of birth, coach notes, raw, average or peak heart rate, or anyone else's data.
  • Your access is checked on every request. The assistant sees only what your Movena role and locations allow, and if your access changes, the connection follows it.
  • The assistant can only read. It can't change bookings, memberships, payments or training records.
  • Movena keeps a record of your consent and of each access or disclosure, never the content that was shared.
  • Answers are information, not medical advice.

Each assistant and each choice is separate

  • Approving one assistant never approves the other. A ChatGPT approval is not reused for Claude, or the other way round.
  • My training and My session health are separate choices. Both start off, and you can withdraw either at any time.
  • A connection lasts one hour. After that, the assistant asks you to connect again.
  • The assistant may ask you to confirm each report or help request, because each one is recorded in your gym's audit trail.

Disconnect

In Movena, open Connections to withdraw either member category, or choose Disconnect to end the connection. Withdrawing stops future sharing. It can't delete what has already been sent: Anthropic or OpenAI handles that under your account plan, settings and their policies. You can also remove Movena in the assistant's own settings.
