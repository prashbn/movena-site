01Money

Memberships, packs and the awkward parts of billing.

Selling a membership is the easy half. Movena is built for the other half — the refund, the make-good, the member who wants to pay at the desk, the plan that has to change.

Plans and packs

Build a membership or a class pack once and sell it everywhere it applies.

  • Recurring memberships — weekly, fortnightly, monthly
  • Class packs — a fixed number of sessions, used down over time
  • What it covers — which class types, which locations
  • Guided setup — three steps: basics, what's included, payment

Taking payment

Online through Stripe, or in person when a member prefers it — the membership is tracked either way.

  • Stripe Connect — payments settle to your own account
  • In person — track the membership, collect the money yourself
  • Card on file — update a member's card from the desk
  • Failed payments — a recovery list, not a mystery

When something goes wrong

The operations most gym software leaves you to fix by hand.

  • Refunds — full or partial, recorded against the member
  • Make-goods — credit a member when your gym got it wrong
  • Cancellations — with the cooling-off rules handled properly
  • Receipts — sent to the member, kept on the record

Knowing where you stand

The numbers an owner actually asks for, without exporting anything.

  • Revenue and churn — what came in, who left
  • Monthly movement — new, upgrades, downgrades, lost
  • Outstanding — who owes, and how long it's been
  • Failures and disputes — surfaced, not buried in Stripe
02The day

The timetable, the door, and everything between.

Timetable and bookings

Build the week once, then let it run.

  • Templates — a repeating week generated weeks ahead
  • Capacity and waitlists — promotion happens automatically
  • Cut-offs — how late a member can book or cancel
  • Rooms and locations — filtered to the site you're standing in

Check-in

A tablet at the door does the front-desk work.

  • Self check-in — members mark themselves in
  • Session picker — the right class, even when two overlap
  • Grace window — late arrivals still count
  • Staff marking — for the times a kiosk won't do

Waivers

Signed before the first session, stored for as long as you need it.

  • Your own wording — versioned, so you know what was agreed
  • Intake questions — ask what you need at sign-up
  • Signed on kiosk or phone — with a real signature
  • Per-location entity — the right business name on each document

Messaging

A private line between your team and your members — never member-to-member.

  • Threads — one conversation per member, with your staff
  • Broadcasts — tell everyone the 6am is cancelled
  • Reports and moderation — hide a message, suspend a sender
  • Push notifications — so it actually gets read
03Growth

The CRM half: enquiries in, members kept.

A gym's pipeline is short and personal — someone walks in, or fills in a form, and somebody has to follow up. Movena keeps that honest without turning your desk into a sales floor.

Leads and enquiries

Everyone who asked about joining, in one list.

  • Enquiry form for your website — drops straight into the pipeline
  • Where they came from — walk-in, referral, website, campaign
  • Status history — who moved it, and when
  • Convert to member — without retyping a thing

Members and guests

The record behind every conversation at the desk.

  • Invites — one email, and they're in the app
  • Guest passes — trials and drop-ins tracked properly
  • Profiles and notes — what your team needs, where they need it
  • Access control — pause or remove access without losing history

Keeping members

Recognition that arrives on its own, plus a nudge when someone stops.

  • Milestone badges — 50, 100, 250, 500, 1,000 classes
  • Challenges — attendance, minutes, or streaks, scored from check-ins
  • Rewards queue — who's owed the shirt, and whether it's been handed over
  • Retention list — members who haven't trained in a fortnight

Programming

What your coaches write, delivered to the people doing it.

  • Workout builder — sets, reps, loads, notes
  • Exercise catalogue — thousands of movements, ready to use
  • Member programs — assigned, with a start date that waits
  • Straight to the app — no PDFs, no printouts
04Your team

Multiple sites, without multiple systems.

Movena was built for a group before it was built for a single site. Locations aren't a setting bolted on later — they run through roles, plans, timetables, reporting and the documents members sign.

A coach at one site sees that site. A manager sees the locations they run. An owner sees everything. Nobody has to remember which login they're in.
