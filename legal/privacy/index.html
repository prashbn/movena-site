Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 June 2026

This policy explains what personal information the Movena member app collects, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. It covers the app on both iOS and Android.

1. Who we are

The Movena member app is operated by Kindness and Wellness (ABN 95 894 823 689) ("Movena", "we", "us"). It serves gyms and their members. For any privacy question or request, contact us at privacy@movena.com.au.

2. The information we collect

We collect only what the app needs to work. We do not use advertising or third-party analytics SDKs, advertising identifiers, or tracking/fingerprinting.

Account & identity

  • Your name and email address (your sign-in identity).
  • An authentication token, kept securely on your device to keep you signed in.
  • Your account may also include your date of birth, phone, or postal address where your gym holds them.

Your gym membership

  • The gym(s) you belong to, your current gym, and your membership plan (name, price, billing interval, period progress). Your data is gym-scoped.

Bookings & activity

  • Classes/sessions you book, booking status and history, and notifications about them.

Messages

  • Messages between you and your gym, including content and read state. Message content is treated as sensitive and is never stored unencrypted on your device.

Performance & fitness data

  • Your personal records and logged workouts, and the training programs your gym assigns you. See section 5 for how we treat this.

Payments

  • When you buy a pack or update your renewal card in the app, your card details are entered into and processed by Stripe (our payment processor) — Movena never sees or stores your full card number. We retain non-sensitive purchase information (e.g. which pack/plan, amount, date).

Photos

  • If you set a profile photo (on iOS or Android), we store the image you choose.

Device & technical data

  • Device model, operating-system and app version, and a push-notification token (to deliver notifications). For account security we record sign-in metadata on our servers (IP address, approximate country, device/app version). Crash and diagnostic data may be handled by the platform (Apple/Google) — we do not use a third-party crash or analytics SDK.

3. How we use your information

  • To provide the app: your gym, bookings, messages, membership, performance history, profile, and payments.
  • To send notifications you've allowed (e.g. waitlist promotions, booking updates).
  • To process purchases and keep your renewal card up to date (via Stripe).
  • To keep your account secure and to provide support.

We process this information to provide the service you've asked for and, where required, with your consent (given at sign-up; you can manage it in the app and your device settings).

4. How we share your information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with advertisers or analytics companies. We share it only:

  • With your gym — your gym, and the coaches who actually train you, can see the context for the members they train, and nothing wider.
  • With service providers acting on our behalf (bound to our instructions): Stripe (payment processing), Google Firebase (push notifications on Android) and Apple (push notifications on iOS), and our cloud hosting provider (Google Cloud, Sydney).
  • Where required by law, or to protect rights and safety.

5. Health & fitness data, and device integrations

Performance data is intimate, and we treat it that way — with boundaries written into the product:

  • Member-owned history. Your training record is yours and moves with you.
  • Gym-scoped visibility. Coaches see context only for members they train.
  • Informational only. What you see is calculated from what was logged — no black box.

The fitness data we log in-app (personal records, logged workouts) is what you record in Movena or your gym provides.

Google Health Connect (Android): with your permission, Movena connects to Google Health Connect to read your heart rate, active energy, and resting heart rate for the classes you book. You grant this explicitly — it is never on by default — and can disconnect at any time in Health Connect or in Movena's settings.

Your raw heart rate and resting heart rate stay on your device — they are never uploaded. From them, Movena computes a per-session effort summary on your device (your average and peak heart rate, an effort band, time in heart-rate zones, active energy, and a data-quality note). Your date of birth is used on your device only to estimate your maximum heart rate, and is not uploaded as part of this.

Sharing with your gym is a separate choice. If you turn on Aggregated health insights in Privacy & Data Sharing, the derived effort summary — not your raw data — is shared with your gym so your coach can see your session effort. You can turn this off at any time. Raw health data is never shared with gyms, and we never sell health or fitness data or share it with advertisers. We keep the derived summaries to provide your history and improve Movena's features; you can request deletion of your account and data (section 7).

Apple Health (HealthKit, iOS): Movena does not currently connect to Apple Health. If we offer that, it will follow the same opt-in, on-device, raw-data-stays-local approach.

What Movena will not do
  • No medical advice
  • No injury prediction
  • No training prescription
  • No readiness scores
  • No black-box claims
  • No selling member data

6. Data retention

We keep your information while your membership/account is active and as needed to provide the service. You can ask us to delete your account and personal information at any time by emailing privacy@movena.com.au; we'll action it within a reasonable period — except for records we're required to keep by law (for example, payment records for tax/accounting). After that, we delete or de-identify your information.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Notifications: manage or turn off push notifications in your device settings.
  • Profile photo: change or remove it in the app.
  • Access, correction, and account deletion: email privacy@movena.com.au to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account and personal information. There is no in-app self-serve deletion yet — we handle requests by email and action them within a reasonable period.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under privacy law.

8. Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information — encrypted transport, secure on-device storage of your session token, and never storing card numbers (handled by Stripe). No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data.

9. Children

The app is intended for adults: you must be at least 18 years old to use it. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. Where a member under 18 trains at a gym, their membership is managed by a parent or guardian who holds the account.

10. International transfers

Your information is hosted in Australia (Google Cloud, Sydney region). Some service providers may process limited information overseas — for example Stripe (payments) and Apple/Google (push notifications). Where information is processed overseas, we take steps to ensure it remains protected.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. We'll post the updated version here and change the "Last updated" date; significant changes will be communicated in-app or by email.
