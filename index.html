03Staying power

The part that keeps members.

Members rarely quit in a moment — they drift. Movena gives your gym the things that make training feel worth showing up for, and a quiet way to notice when someone stops.

Recognition that lands by itself

Members earn a digital badge at 50, 100, 250, 500 and 1,000 classes — automatically, and kept in their app for good. Your desk sees who just crossed one, so someone can say something.

Challenges the whole gym can join

Run a challenge on classes attended, minutes trained, or the longest streak. Scores come from check-ins, so nobody has to log anything. Set a finish line and everyone who reaches it earns a finisher badge.

A nudge before someone drifts

A retention list surfaces members who haven't trained in a fortnight, so your team can reach out while it still matters — and note what they did about it.

05Data & privacy

Members own their story.

A training history belongs to the person who trained it. Movena is built on that boundary — it's the architecture, not a policy page.

Portable by design

A member's performance journey can follow them. Sharing it with another gym is theirs to grant, never the gym's to take.

Consent, not defaults

Nothing about a member is shared without them saying so, and consent is asked in plain language — per gym, never bundled.

Erasure honoured

When someone leaves and asks to be forgotten, deletion is designed in — mapped table by table, not retrofitted later.
