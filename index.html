07 · TRUST

Boundaries from day one.

Performance data is intimate. Movena treats it that way — with explicit boundaries the platform refuses to cross, written into the architecture before the first feature ships.

  • Member-owned performance history
    Your record of training is yours. It moves with you.
  • Gym-scoped coach visibility
    Coaches see context for the members they actually train. Nothing wider.
  • Consent-first future integrations
    Wearables and devices connect on explicit member consent — never by default.
  • Calculated from logged data, informational only
    What you see is the math on what was logged. No black box. No claims beyond that.
What Movena will not do
  • No medical advice
  • No injury prediction
  • No training prescription
  • No readiness scores
  • No black-box claims
  • No selling member data
