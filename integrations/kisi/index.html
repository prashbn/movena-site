Movena + Kisi

Connect Movena to Kisi and a membership becomes door access. Members who hold the right plan walk in — and access is granted and revoked automatically as memberships change, at every location you cover.

What the integration does

  • Memberships open doors. Put facility access on a plan and its members can enter while their membership is active — no fobs to hand out, no lists to maintain.
  • Bookings open doors too. A confirmed class booking grants access for the session window on its own, so class-based plans work naturally without around-the-clock entry.
  • Changes take care of themselves. Suspensions, holds, cancellations and expiries remove access automatically; reactivation brings it back. Turning a location off removes access there and nothing else.
  • Multi-location by design. Each participating location gets its own place and member group in your Kisi organization, and one person is one Kisi user across all of them — no duplicate seats.

Who decides what

Movena decides who has access, from memberships, bookings and staff decisions. Kisi decides when and which doors — opening hours, door schedules and the hardware itself stay in the Kisi console.

Two switches, both in Movena, control standing access: Include facility access on the membership plan, and Door access on the member. Nobody gets access automatically — a member participates only after your team turns them on.

Setting it up

  1. Ask Movena to activate the Access Control add-on for your locations.
  2. In Movena, go to Settings → Access Control, paste an API key created by your Kisi administrator, and choose Connect. Movena verifies the key with Kisi before saving it, and stores it encrypted.
  3. Choose Configure Access Control. Movena creates a place and a member group in your Kisi organization for each location and sets up live event updates — there are no IDs to copy between systems.
  4. In the Kisi console, register your hardware (controller and reader), create your doors, and add each door to the group Movena created — Movena Members — [location name].
  5. Choose Turn on for the location. Movena shows how many members will receive access and roughly how many Kisi seats that uses before you confirm.

What you need

  • A Kisi organization with an active subscription, installed Kisi hardware, and enough user seats for the members who'll hold access.
  • The Movena Access Control add-on, active for your participating locations.
  • Owner or manager access to your gym's settings in Movena.

Built to be trusted with your doors

  • Continuous reconciliation. Changes apply within about a minute, Kisi's live events are processed as they arrive, and a nightly verification re-checks everything and repairs drift.
  • Movena manages only what Movena created. Users, groups and access set up directly in Kisi are never modified or removed.
  • Capacity checked first. Any change that would add Kisi users in bulk shows its seat impact up front — and is refused rather than half-applied if seats run short.
  • Credentials protected. Your API key is verified, encrypted at rest, and never shown again; event updates from Kisi are individually signed and verified.
