04Yours

Your training history belongs to you.

Not to a single gym. Consent, portability and deletion were designed in from the start.

It can follow you

Change gyms, keep the history. Yours to share. Yours to withdraw.

Nothing shared quietly

Asked in plain language, per gym. Never bundled. Health data is optional and separate.

Leaving means leaving

Ask us to delete your information and we do. Where a record has to be kept, we tell you which, and why. Write to us.
