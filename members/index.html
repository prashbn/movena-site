01Every day

The bits you'll use constantly.

Book a class

See what's on, book anything your membership covers, and join the waitlist when it's full — if a spot opens you're moved up automatically and told about it.

Check in

Mark yourself in on the tablet at the door on your way past. Arriving a little late still counts.

Follow your program

If your coach has assigned you a program, the day's training is in the app — every movement, set and load laid out.

Message your gym

A private thread with your gym's team — not other members, and nothing public. Report or block a message any time.

Finisher badge earned

Milestones you didn't have to chase.

Turn up, and the recognition finds you. Movena counts every class you attend at your gym and awards a badge at 50, 100, 250, 500 and 1,000 — automatically, and yours to keep. Some gyms hand over something real at those marks too.

Join a gym challenge and the leaderboard takes care of itself: your check-ins are the score, so there's nothing to log. Reach the finish line and you earn a finisher badge the moment you cross it.

03The long view

A history that builds.

What your coach records in a session becomes your record, not just the gym's. Movements you train often, the sessions behind them, and the shape of your training over months — described plainly, never dressed up as advice.

Moments worth keeping

When you match or beat a previous best, it's marked — a moment rather than a number buried in a list.

Movements you know

The lifts you train regularly, with their history — so you can see what's actually changed since autumn.

Your own pace

No scores out of ten, no rankings against strangers, no telling you what you should have done.

04Yours

Your training history belongs to you.

Not to the gym you happened to join. That principle shaped how Movena is built, and it's why the boring parts — consent, portability, deletion — were designed in from the start rather than added when someone asked.

It can follow you

Change gyms and your journey doesn't have to start again. Sharing it with a new gym is yours to grant — and yours to withdraw.

Nothing shared quietly

You're asked in plain language, per gym, and never in a bundle you can't unpick. Health data is optional and separate.

Leaving means leaving

Ask to be forgotten and deletion is real, not a hidden flag on a row. Write to us and we'll handle it.
